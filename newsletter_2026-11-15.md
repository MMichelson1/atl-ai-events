## Question of the Week, answer

A bench. One person holding everything is a single point of failure, and Nvidia just showed what happens when that point fails: a sudden departure, no named successor, no transition plan, and a board left to name an interim CEO and search for a permanent one. The fix is simple and it is boring: never let one person be the only one who knows how things run. That is why every AI Collective chapter needs at least three people: a lead who acts like the president, and organizers who act like a vice president and a treasurer, so the chapter keeps going no matter what happens to any one person. If your chapter is a one-person show, this is the week to recruit person number two and person number three.

Source: Platformer, Nov 13, 2026; Nvidia SEC filings, which warn that ineffective succession planning could hinder strategy and execution.
