Hi everyone!

November is the month we say thank you, and I want to start there. Thank you for showing up, for helping each other, and for making this community what it is. Helping and gratitude go hand in hand for me. We help because we are grateful, and we are grateful because we help.

That is what the Collective is. In every chapter, there are people ready to help each other. Our organizers teach free classes. We host coffees, lunches, hikes, dinners, workshops, and demo nights. If you are new, come to one. If you have been around a while, bring someone with you.

I want to tell you about a tool that has been helping me. It is called Muse, Meta's new free personal AI agent. I have been using it for a couple of weeks and it has been pretty amazing. You control security and approve actions, and it has cut at least 20 hours of weekly work for me. If you want to try it, redeem the code ZIWJYA in Settings within 48 hours of joining and you will get 1 billion Muse tokens. Here is the link: https://muse.ai/join

We have two big nights coming up. This Wednesday, November 11, Tim Rayburn is at Improving in Alpharetta for AI 201, a hands-on workshop on how to think about AI. Then on Wednesday, November 18, we have Bring Your Agent to Work Night at Tech North in Alpharetta. Nick Cybela is hosting, I am organizing, and six of you will demo something you are doing with an agent. Come see what agents can really do. Want to demo? Sign up in advance here: demo sign-up form

And if you are in another chapter, take this idea home. Host a demo night of your own. Or a vibe night, a coffee, a hike, a lunch. The best events we do are low cost or no cost. Pick something simple, pick a date, and make it yours.

I am working on two ideas for early next year, and I need your help with both. First, a pitch night for founders. We want to pull together accredited investors and founders who are looking for their next level of funding. If you know an investor or a founder, get in touch. Second, a job fair. We want recruiters and hiring companies there, plus people to help you use AI for your resume and your job hunt, and to use agents to keep track of applications and follow-ups. If either of these speaks to you, let me know.

We also need sponsors. We need financial sponsors and in-kind sponsors for events, food and drink, venues, and more. There is a budget for every wallet. And if your business would like to be on one of our chapter banners, I would love to have a chat with you. Reach out and we will talk.

And we need volunteers. Join the team if you want to help: https://aicollective.com/join-the-team Or host an event in your neighborhood. It could be a coffee, a hike, a lunch, anything. Make it simple. Maybe once a quarter. It is your event, and we will help you put it together.

Thank you for being part of this. Be sure to check this newsletter each week to find in-person AI events in your area from the AI Collective and other organizations across the Southeast.

Cheers,

Mark
