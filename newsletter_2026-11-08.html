Question of the Week, answer
It doesn't just talk to you, it does things for you. A chatbot answers and stops. An agent browses, clicks, drafts emails, and keeps a virtual computer running on your behalf, and every action costs real compute. Grok Bot reached 800,000 paying users in two months, and even at $30 to $200 a month, serving it cost more than the subscriptions brought in. The companies sold agents cheap to grow fast, then rationed the usage once the bills came due. Same money, less agent. If you build your work around an agent, remember you're renting someone else's computer, and they can change the deal.
Source: TechCrunch, Nov 2, 2026. Musk, Nov 1, 2026: "The cost of serving Grok Bot was unsustainably high relative to revenue."
