Which one do I use?

Color on white or light backgrounds. White on orange, slate, blue, or a photo. Black for one-color printing, forms, and sponsor paperwork.

Step 3. Make your first card

  1. Your Chapter

    Type your Chapter name and pick it from the list. Your member numbers fill in for you. Leave "Pick the strongest one for me" on. It uses your Chapter number if it is big enough, and the Region or network number if it is not. Your name, title, and contact go at the end of every post. Leave them blank and leads go to the regional office: Mark Michelson, mark@aicollective.com or Calendly.com/michelson. Fill in your own if you want leads to come straight to you.

  2. Logo and picture

    Your White and Color logos load automatically when you pick your Chapter. If your Chapter is not in the list, email mark@aicollective.com for your logo set, then load the White logo in the first box and the Color logo in the second. Then add your event photo, or tap Use the standard photo.

  3. Background

    Pick a background. Orange, slate, and blue all work with the White logo. White uses the Color logo.

  4. The sentence

    Pick a message. Each one names your city. Swap it each time you post so your feed does not repeat.

  5. Sizes

    Choose the sizes for where you post. Square for LinkedIn and Luma. IG portrait for the Instagram feed. Story for Instagram and Facebook Stories.

  6. Export

    Tap Download this image. On a phone, confirm the save. The card lands in your Photos or Downloads.

  7. Copy the post text

    Scroll down below the preview to Now copy the post text. You get four versions written to back up the sentence on your card, and they change when you change the sentence. Pick one and tap Copy. Each one ends with the contact line.

Never post the card alone. The card gets attention. The post text makes the case and tells people how to reach you. Every post needs both.

Step 4. Post it

Start a new post. Attach your card, then paste the post text you copied. Post on LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, or wherever your people are. Two or three times a week is the goal.

The cards cover annual sponsorship. Nonprofit donor leads are welcome too. Banks, colleges, universities, and law firms often need a nonprofit to give to. Send those leads to the regional office now. Our 501(c)(3) should be set up by the end of November, and we invoice donors once it is. Nonprofit gifts do not earn commission. A stipend program for them is coming. Do not promise a tax deduction before then.

Step 5. Close the deal, your way

Commission tops out at 15% of a commercial sponsorship, every time. Who earns it depends on who does the work.

Lane 1. Hand it to the regional office: you earn 5%

Email mark@aicollective.com with the lead's name, email, phone, company, title, and LinkedIn URL. The regional office does the closing and earns the other 10%. You earn 5% when the deposit lands.

The regional office also goes after larger companies that touch several Chapters, like regional and national banks. If you know someone at a bank, university, law firm, or any big local employer, send it in. Even an introduction helps.

Lane 2. Close it yourself: you earn the full 15%

You handle it start to finish in Sponsor Studio: the proposal, the invoice, and the follow-up until the deposit lands. Ask the regional office for Sponsor Studio access.
